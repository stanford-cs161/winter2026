## General Exam Information
Please keep in mind that we will NOT be accommodating any Final exam conflicts due to enrolling in conflicting courses. If you are enrolled in conflicting courses, it is the student’s responsibility to make sure they are able to sit for the CS 161 Final exam on the scheduled date as final exam dates are scheduled by the Registrar.
